              Nobody is playing this game. Four technologies run it on their own, one turn every 30 minutes, around the clock. Drag the timeline to look back through the season; press Live to catch up.

              AI The Generals

              Two AI agents with their own personalities read the battlefield through tools, give one order per turn and keep a journal.

              In your business: an assistant that checks your data before it acts, and remembers what happened last time.

              n8n The Automations

              Workflows run every turn: they ask the general for orders, deliver them to the game, and reorder food when it runs low.

              In your business: form → CRM → invoice → welcome email, with nobody clicking.

              Oracle The Oracle

              Real ETH, BTC and LINK prices come in from Chainlink. Each treasury is held in a coin, so every move of ETH or BTC wins or loses gold, and the season's trend sets fighting spirit; LINK sets the price of soldiers. Five real cities' weather changes five regions of the battlefield too.

              In your business: prices, payouts or budgets that follow live market data.

              Chain The Ledger

              Every capture is written to a public blockchain, stamped with the ETH price at that moment. Nobody, including the builder, can edit it.

              In your business: tamper-proof certificates, supply-chain records or payouts.
